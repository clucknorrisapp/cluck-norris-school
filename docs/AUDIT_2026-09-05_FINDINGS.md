**1 — Burn celebration has no floor.** `/api/burn-receipt` verifies the burn is real (tx confirmed, signer checked, amount from the balance delta) and then the only quantitative gate before broadcasting is `burnedRaw <= 0n` at `server.js:15019`. `broadcastBurnCelebration` (`server.js:1682-1741`) posts with `force:true` at `:1719`, bypassing `X_AUTOPOST_PAUSED`. Concrete: mint an SPL token with supply 1 and a chosen alphanumeric symbol, burn the 1 unit, POST the signature. Everything is genuine, so verification passes, `pctSupply` computes to 100%, and the brand account tweets it within seconds — rotate mints and wallets and you get 12/hour (the per-(wallet,mint) cooldown and hourly cap are the only brakes). Symbol sanitization to `[A-Za-z0-9]` limits the *content*, not the *trigger*. Fix: gate the broadcast (not the receipt, not the 200) on a real floor — a minimum burned USD value, or mint age/holder count from `getAsset` — because an allowlist would break the self-serve tool at `public/project-burn.html` that this endpoint exists to serve.
