## THE finding (don't re-derive this — it cost a day)

**The bosses look "sunk / waist-deep / legs under the pavement" because the boss cutout art is drawn
from the THIGHS UP — there are no feet.** The boss's *position is correct* (body.bottom sits exactly
on `GY`, verified by measurement); the sprite simply has no feet to show, so its legs terminate at the
pavement with nothing below — which reads as buried, worst of all right next to Normie, whose art has
real shoes on the ground.

Hard evidence (opaque-pixel bounds of each texture, fraction of texture height where content ends):

| texture (src/assets/) | content bottom | feet drawn? |
|---|---|---|
| `cut_normie_idle.b64` (player) | 99.1% | ✅ shoes at the very bottom |
| `cut_jeet.b64` (the green goblin) | 100% | ✅ |
| `cut_bot.b64` (enemy) | 100% | ✅ |
| `cut_scammykol.b64` (KOL boss) | **96.4% — cut off at the thighs** | ❌ no feet |
| `cut_troll.b64` (boss) | 95.6% | ❌ |
| `cut_ceoboss.b64` (boss) | 97.2% | ❌ |

**Every "cut_" BOSS cutout has this. Enemies and the player do not.** This is why 24 hours of floor
clamps, penBoss tweaks, body-offset changes, and position measurements never moved it — the position
was always right. Do not chase position again.

**How to re-measure any texture:** decode the `.b64` (strip any `data:` prefix, base64-decode to PNG),
then in a headless canvas find the min/max opaque rows. The boss/player art has been this way since
the repo's git baseline (5f80105, 2026-08-12) — it is NOT a recent regression, but it IS a real bug.

## Tools / gotchas for continuing

- **Build:** `node normie-quest/src/build.js` inlines `src/game_logic.js` + swaps `__MARKER__` art
  tokens for the `.b64` data-URIs, writing `public/normie-quest-platformer.html` (+ `-play.html`).
- **Serve locally:** `PORT=3111 node server.js` (serves the built HTML fresh each request — no restart
  needed after a rebuild). Lab lane: `/normie-quest-x7-lab`.
- **Reach the scene in headless:** `window.Phaser.GAMES` is **undefined** in this build. Use only the
  `window.__NQ_*` hooks (`__NQ_STARTLEVEL`, `__NQ_FORCEBOSS`, `__NQ_BOSSVIEW`, `__NQ_SHOVEBOSS`,
  `__NQ_DBG`, `__NQ_RECT`). `__NQ_RECT('boss'|'player'|'<texkey>')` returns screen-fraction rects
  (feet = `y+h`) — the same probe the visual gate uses.
- **Headless does NOT settle a forced boss's fall** — it stays at spawn `y`. After `__NQ_FORCEBOSS`,
  call `__NQ_SHOVEBOSS(0,222)` to seat the KOL on the floor (body.bottom→GY), then screenshot.
- `nq-visual.cjs` and `nq-verify.cjs` are **slow / hang** in this heavy-headless container (spawn ~12
  browsers). `node normie-quest/test/nq-geometry-check.cjs` is fast and static — use it. The visual
  gate's only HARD gate is the gravemite; character/HUD surfaces are advisory (flaky).
- **Constants:** `W=480 H=270 TILE=24 GY=246` (floor top). Boss depth 7, player 7, shadow 6, floor 0,
  bgArt −58.
