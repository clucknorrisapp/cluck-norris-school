In one minute

Depth, not trades

The engine never buys its own token to fake activity. It places liquidity and gets filled by other people. If nobody trades, the volume is zero — which is exactly what the paused fleet shows below.

Multi-quote dislocation

Token/USDC, token/SOL and token/JUP pools each move with their quote. Every 1% move re-centres a pool and hands an arbitrageur a real trade. That is where the organic flow comes from.

The score is rented

Jupiter's organic score decays to zero without runtime. CLKN reads it today. A stopped engine that still showed a high score would be the thing to distrust.

The hard questions

Is this wash trading?

No. The engine posts two-sided depth and is filled by third parties. The split above shows Jupiter's own tagging of organic versus router flow; router flow is greyed exactly as Jupiter greys it. Routing volume is bought with real depth at one or two basis points — and it is the arbitrageur who trades, not us.

Who signs?

A per-project operator hot wallet that holds only that client's float. Never a mint authority, never a freeze authority, never the treasury for a client token.

Is it custody?

Yes, and we call it that. The client sends the float, the engine may sell absorbed inventory back to recoup its quote side by design, and wind-down returns what is there. It is a managed service, not a protocol.

Is the score bought?

Rented. It rises while real depth is live and it decays when the engine stops. The trajectory panel is that claim on live data.

Why is everything paused?

The owner's call on 2026-09-05: no engine runs for any project until this dashboard shows the record in the open. The stop is an environment flag and a code default, not a database row, so a redeploy cannot un-pause it.
