For Projects

ONE GUIDED PATH · EVERY TOOL WE RUN

Cluck Norris runs on Educate → Build → Earn: teach people the truth about crypto, build with other projects and communities instead of only for ourselves, and make the earning side honest and provable. This page is the front door for a project team — the tools already live, in the order most teams actually reach for them, plus a check of what your own token has already done.
The guided flow
1
Lock your tokens
Put team or treasury supply into a public, on-chain lock — free, non-custodial, audited (Jupiter Lock). Usually the first thing a community asks a project to prove.
OPEN THE LOCKER ROOM →
2
Run a lock-to-earn program
Reward the holders who lock, from a public program record anyone can check — the rules, who qualified, and the transaction that paid each one. Apply and we review it by hand.
APPLY FOR LOCK TO EARN →
3
Run a buy competition
Reward every buyer a % of what they bought, or rank the top buyers for prizes — on-chain buyer discovery plus a hold-verified no-sells check, then one click to the airdropper.
RUN A COMPETITION →
4
Send an airdrop
Batch-send tokens to hundreds of wallets — automatic token-account creation, size-safe batching, an exportable record with Solscan links. Your keys never leave your wallet.
SEND AN AIRDROP →
5
Get your listings right
See what CoinGecko, GeckoTerminal, DexScreener, Jupiter and the rest actually show for your contract — correct, wrong or not found, field by field, with the exact page to fix each one.
CHECK YOUR LISTINGS →
6
Know your holder list
A wallet-by-wallet snapshot of who holds your token — LP pools and lock escrows separated out, supply concentration, and an exportable CSV of recipient candidates.
RUN A SNAPSHOT →
7
Burn supply, on purpose, with proof
Destroy part of your own supply as a tokenomics event and get a shareable, on-chain-verified receipt for your community. Non-custodial, no cut.
BURN WITH A RECEIPT →
Check your project
Paste your token's mint. We re-read the public record for each of these — no wallet, nothing saved — and link straight to whichever step you haven't done yet.
