🛟 LP RESCUE

Can't find your liquidity? Search the blockchain directly.

Sometimes a real LP position doesn't show up in a DEX website — a newer pool misses the search index, a portfolio page comes up blank. It happens on every DEX, and the funds are always still on-chain. LP Rescue reads Solana directly, shows you exactly what exists, and if it's yours, builds a withdrawal that your own wallet signs. Free, for everyone.

🔒 Cluck Norris never asks for your seed phrase or private key. LP Rescue reads public blockchain data and creates transactions that are reviewed and signed directly by your wallet. Connecting a wallet does not give Cluck Norris control over your funds.
STEP 1 — SCAN (no wallet needed)
Fill in whatever you know — any one field is enough. Wallet only = scan every DLMM pool. Wallet + pool is the fastest and most precise.
⛓️ Chain is truth
Websites index the blockchain, and any index can lag or miss a pool. Solana itself is the only source consulted here for ownership and balances.
🔑 Non-custodial
Withdrawals go only to the position owner's own wallet, which reviews and signs every transaction itself. We take nothing and can touch nothing.
🩺 Real diagnosis
Not just "found / not found" — you get the why: not yet indexed by the app, wrong wallet, locked, empty, or a scan failure that says so honestly.

LP Rescue is a blockchain interface and diagnostic tool. It does not guarantee that a position can be recovered — some positions are locked, empty, owned by another wallet, or otherwise unrecoverable. Always review wallet transactions before signing. Your wallet will display one or more approval requests during a withdrawal; read them.
