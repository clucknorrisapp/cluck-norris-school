FIREPIT

Burn junk tokens. Reclaim your SOL. We take nothing.

Old airdrops and dead tokens leave empty accounts on your wallet, each holding a little SOL rent (~0.002 SOL) that's yours to reclaim. Firepit burns the junk and closes the accounts so that SOL comes back to you — non-custodial, open-source, and with a hard guard so you never torch a bag that's still worth money.

🔑 You sign, we can't touch it
Your wallet signs a transaction you can read. We never take custody, never ask for approvals or delegate rights.
💰 We take zero cut
100% of the reclaimed SOL rent goes back to you. Not a skim, not a fee.
🛡️ Value guard
Every token is priced live. Anything still worth money is flagged, and you must confirm the exact dollar value before it burns.
✅ Only what you pick
Nothing is pre-selected. Firepit only ever touches the accounts you tick — never a blind "burn everything."
