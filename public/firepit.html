FIREPIT

Burn junk tokens. Reclaim your SOL. We take nothing.

Old airdrops and dead tokens leave empty accounts on your wallet, each holding a little SOL rent (~0.002 SOL) that's yours to reclaim. Firepit burns the junk and closes the accounts so that SOL comes back to you — non-custodial, open-source, and with a hard guard so you never torch a bag that's still worth money.

🔑 You sign, we can't touch it
Your wallet signs a transaction you can read. We never take custody, never ask for approvals or delegate rights.
💰 We take zero cut
100% of the reclaimed SOL rent goes back to you. Not a skim, not a fee.
🛡️ Value guard
Every token is priced live. Anything still worth money is flagged, and you must confirm the exact dollar value before it burns.
✅ Only what you pick
Nothing is pre-selected. Firepit only ever touches the accounts you tick — never a blind "burn everything."

🎯 A PROJECT OWNER?

Firepit is for clearing worthless junk. If you want to deliberately burn part of your own token's supply as a tokenomics event — where destroying value is the point — use Project Burn. Burn any amount, keep your account open, and get a shareable, on-chain-verified receipt for your community.

🔥 Open Project Burn

Firepit never burns tokens with unknown value: NFTs are listed but not yet burnable here (floor-price safety is coming). Frozen or restricted accounts can't be closed and are shown greyed out.

🛡️ Why it's safe: every transaction is built right here in your browser from just two instruction types — burn the tokens you ticked, then close the emptied account so its rent lands back in your own wallet. No token approvals, no delegates, no fee instruction, and nowhere for the SOL to go except to you. Your wallet shows you exactly that before you approve — and if you don't sign, nothing happens.
