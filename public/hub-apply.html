Run Lock to Earn for your token

YOUR RULES · JUPITER LOCK · RECEIPTS ANYONE CAN CHECK

Reward the holders who lock your token on Jupiter Lock. Nobody holds their tokens — not you, not us: the escrow is Jupiter's own program. You set the rules below. Our engine reads the chain every hour, works out who qualifies and what they have earned, and pays from your funding wallet on the schedule you choose. Every payout has a transaction any holder can check on the public page.

This is staking rewards without a staking contract — no contract to audit, no tokens to custody, no risk of a drain. What it costs: 0.5 SOL a month (or 0.25 SOL for a small-cap project), payable in SOL or in CLKN priced at the moment you pay. The reward tokens are yours and stay in your wallet until they are paid out.

Rewards leave this wallet only when a payout batch is signed. Its balance is shown publicly next to what the program owes, so holders can see whether the rewards are covered. Locks from this wallet never earn.

STEP 3 — THE RULES

Every rule becomes part of a versioned, hashed program the holders can read. Changing a rule later creates a new version; periods already accrued keep the old one.

A lock shorter than the minimum does not qualify. A longer commitment weighs more, from 1× at the minimum up to the top tier — locks longer than the top tier count as the top tier.

A cancelable lock can be taken back early by its creator, so it is not the commitment a fixed lock is.

Whatever the shape, only what is still locked earns.

Split every hour between everyone who qualifies, by locked amount × term weight. This is what the program owes per day and what the funding wallet must cover — never a rate promised to anyone.

The funding wallet is always excluded. Add treasury and team wallets so their locks do not take from the community's pool.

STEP 4 — THE PLAN

Nothing is charged now. Once the owner approves, you pay the first month from your wallet and the program is armed. A project we already work with may be comped — that is the owner's call, not a box to tick.
