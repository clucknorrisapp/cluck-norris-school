A swap only works if the desk holds enough of the side you're buying. It keeps a reserve back, so the last of it is never sold.

ℹ️ How this works

Both tokens are priced in dollars at the moment you swap, and the desk re-checks that price again right before the transaction is built — if the market moves while you're deciding, you get a fresh quote instead of a stale price. The desk takes a small spread, shown above before you commit.

Why doesn't this move the price?

Because it isn't a pool. A normal AMM prices by how much you drain from it, so every trade moves the chart — that's what an AMM is. Here you're trading against a fixed float at market price, so nothing about your swap touches either token's market.

What are you actually signing?

One transaction containing both transfers: yours to the desk, and the desk's to you. It's atomic — if any part fails, the whole thing fails and nothing moves. Your wallet signs first and the desk co-signs after; the desk can only co-sign the exact transaction it quoted you.
