OWNERS SNAPSHOT & HISTORY

Who is in. Who is out. Who is draining you. Every holder over $5, read wallet by wallet — free.

HOW IT WORKS
Paste your token's mint. A background crawler walks every holder worth more than $5, reads each wallet's entire history with your token (bought, sold, received, sent), and reads the pattern: ACCUMULATING · HOLDING · TRIMMING · DRAINING · OUT. It flags bags that were handed out rather than bought, wallets under two weeks old, and it links wallets that share a first funder, pass tokens between each other, or cash out to the same address. It's deliberately slow and cheap — on a big token a run can take hours — so come back, the result is saved. Every run is a snapshot; re-run later and it shows who entered, who left, and who changed.
