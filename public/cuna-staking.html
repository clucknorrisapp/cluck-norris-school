You earn on every token that is locked for 90 days or more, at the rate for how long that token is locked — tokens that unlock sooner simply don’t count. Lock longer and that piece earns at a higher rate, held flat for as long as it stays locked, from the first day to the last. It is set when you lock and it never drops. A lock built straight on Jupiter counts exactly the same way, release by release — only the part locked 90 days or more earns. Rewards build daily and are sent to your wallet in batches — nothing to claim, no deadline to catch.

No bonus table — the terms are 90-day steps and that is just the division. An 18-month lock earns 6× the entry rate on its last day exactly as it did on its first; it never fades to the rate of a shorter lock. If a schedule releases in pieces, each piece is judged on its own — the tokens released early stop earning the moment they unlock, but a tail still locked for 90 days or more keeps earning at its own rate regardless of what already came free. That top tier is also the ceiling — a longer lock made elsewhere is welcome, but is paid at the top-tier rate, not more.

HOW IT’S DECIDED — NOTHING TO SUBMIT

A Jupiter Lock is a public account on Solana. We read the chain and work out who qualifies. You never send us proof, a screenshot, or a signature — nothing you type is an input, so there is nothing anyone can fake.

Rewards work the same way: counted daily, sent in batches. Do nothing after locking and you still get everything you earned.

It also doesn’t matter where you make the lock. Build it here or on Jupiter’s own site — same program, same rewards.

“IS THIS A STAKING CONTRACT?”

No — and that’s the point. A normal staking contract holds your tokens. You send them in and trust the team’s code to give them back. That is where a lot of people have lost everything.

Here, nobody holds your tokens — including us. They sit in a Jupiter Lock escrow with your wallet as the recipient. We only read the chain and count.

So it is staking in the way that matters: you commit tokens, you earn for committing them. It just isn’t a vault you have to trust.

HOW MUCH SHOULD I LOCK?

A slice, not your bag. Pick the amount by what you could genuinely not touch for the whole term and still be fine. If that number is zero, the honest answer is don’t lock — that is a real answer and it costs you nothing.

Ladder it. You can hold several locks at once and they all earn; multiple locks to the same wallet are added together. Lock a piece now and another in a few months, and keep the rest liquid.

Cancelable locks don’t earn here. Jupiter can make one you’re able to undo — a fine thing to make, but a lock you can cancel isn’t a commitment, and paying it the same would mean the people who really locked up earn the least.
