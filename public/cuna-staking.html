You start earning the moment your lock exists. Rewards build up every day and are sent out in batches — nothing to click, nothing to claim, they arrive in your wallet. Your share is your amount × days still to run. That one line is the whole formula: a longer lock earns more per token, and your weight falls as the lock matures, which is why re-locking is worth it rather than being forced on you.

HOW IT’S DECIDED — THERE IS NOTHING TO SUBMIT

A Jupiter Lock is a public account on Solana. We read the chain and work out who qualifies. You never send us proof, a screenshot, or a signature. Nothing you type is an input, so there is nothing anyone can fake.

Rewards work the same way. There is no claim button and no deadline to catch — what you have earned is counted every day and sent to your wallet in batches. If you do nothing at all after locking, you still get everything you earned.

That also means it doesn’t matter where you make the lock. Build it here, or build it yourself on Jupiter’s own site — same program, same result, same rewards.

“IS THIS A STAKING CONTRACT?”

No — and that’s the point. A normal staking contract is a program that holds your tokens. You send them in and trust the team’s code to give them back. That is where a lot of people have lost everything, to bugs and to teams who wrote a withdraw function only they could call.

Here, nobody holds your tokens — including us. They sit in a Jupiter Lock escrow with your wallet as the recipient. Jupiter’s program is audited and used by hundreds of projects, and we could not touch your tokens if we wanted to. We only read the chain and count.

So it is staking, in the way that matters: you commit tokens, you earn for committing them. It just isn’t a vault you have to trust. The minimum is 3 months — short enough to be a real choice, long enough that the commitment means something.

BEFORE YOU LOCK — READ THIS
A qualifying lock cannot be undone. Not by you, not by us, not by Jupiter. Your tokens are gone from your wallet for the full term — three months at the shortest, eighteen at the longest. There is no cancel button, no support ticket, no early exit.

So lock a slice, not your bag. Decide the amount by what you could genuinely not touch for a year and still be fine. If that number is zero, the honest answer is to not lock — that is a real answer and it costs you nothing.

Ladder it instead of going all in. You can hold several locks at once and they all earn; multiple locks to the same wallet are added together. Lock a piece now and another piece in a few months, and you keep the rest liquid for whatever life does in the meantime.

Jupiter Lock can also make a cancelable lock that you can undo. Those are a real and sensible thing to make — but they do not earn here. A lock you can cancel is not a commitment, and paying it the same as a real one would mean the people who genuinely locked up earn the least.
