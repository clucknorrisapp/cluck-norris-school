CLUCK NORRIS · SCHOOL OF CRYPTO HARD KNOCKS · STORE EDITION

PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE: SEPTEMBER 11, 2026
The short version: This is the Cluck Norris app as distributed on Google Play and the App Store — a free crypto school plus two read-only safety tools. No account, no email, no wallet connection, no wallet address, no payments. We never ask for a seed phrase or private key and never take custody of anything. The website at clucknorris.app carries more features and has its own, fuller policy at clucknorris.app/privacy.

This policy applies to the store edition of Cluck Norris (the app installed from Google Play or the App Store), published by CLKN Productions LLC. The app is a separately built, education-only edition: the features described below are the only ones it contains.

What the app is

A free crypto school (lessons, quizzes, the Incubator, the LP Lab, the Library, an AI tutor called “Ask Cluck”, read-aloud, seven languages) plus two read-only research tools: the Wallet Safety Checkup (scan only) and the Listing Checkup. It has no accounts, no purchases, no wallet connection and no on-chain transactions.

Information we collect

  • Lesson progress with an anonymous session id. When you complete a lesson, the app sends the lesson id together with a random identifier it generated on first launch (stored on your device, not tied to your name, email, phone, device serial or advertising id). We keep this so we can issue your certificate of completion against a real record. Sessions idle for 120 days are deleted. The app also sends a small set of feature-usage events (for example “lesson opened”) that we only ever count in aggregate. There is no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK in the app.
  • Your certificate of completion. When the school’s record shows you finished the curriculum, we issue a certificate: a random certificate id, the issue date, the number of lessons and coursework items completed, and a one-way hash of your session id. Anyone with the id can confirm at clucknorris.app/certificate/<id> that a certificate with that id is genuine and when it was issued. The name you type on the certificate stays on your device only — it is never sent to us.
  • Addresses you paste into the research tools. The public Solana wallet address or token you enter into the Wallet Safety Checkup or the Listing Checkup is sent to our server, which queries public blockchain and market data to build the report. These are public blockchain identifiers, sent only when you run a lookup, and not linked to your session id.
  • Questions you ask Ask Cluck. The text you send to the AI tutor (with the lesson you are on, for context) goes to our server and on to our AI provider to generate an answer. Please do not include personal information in a question. If you report an answer, the question, the answer and the reason you pick are stored for our review, without any identifier.
  • Read-aloud. When you tap play, the lesson text is sent to our text-to-speech provider to produce audio. No user data is involved beyond the lesson text itself.
  • Language mode. Choosing a language may send interface text to our AI provider for translation. That is our own copy, not your data.
  • On your device only. Lesson progress, bookmarks, language, read-aloud settings and the certificate name live in the app’s local storage. Clearing the app’s storage removes them.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account.
  • No wallet connection and no wallet address — this edition has no connect, no holder check, no airdrop and no on-chain transactions.
  • No location, contacts, photos, files or device identifiers.
  • No payment or financial-account information — there are no in-app purchases.
  • No seed phrases or private keys — ever.

Who we share data with

Only what a feature needs, and only with providers that run it:

  • Our AI provider (Anthropic) — the Ask Cluck question and lesson context; interface text for translation.
  • Our text-to-speech provider (ElevenLabs) — lesson text for read-aloud, when the service is configured; otherwise the device’s own voice is used and nothing is sent.
  • Blockchain and market-data providers — the address or token you paste, sent to a Solana RPC provider (Helius) for the Wallet Safety Checkup and to public market-data APIs (for example DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, CoinGecko, Solscan, Jupiter’s token list, RugCheck) for the Listing Checkup.

We do not sell data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Security and retention

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Lesson-progress sessions are deleted after 120 days of inactivity. Certificate records are kept so that certificates stay verifiable. Answer reports are kept for review and capped in number. Because the app has no accounts and collects no wallet address, most of what it stores is on your device.

Your choices

Clearing the app’s storage removes your progress, bookmarks and certificate name from the device. To ask us to delete the server-side lesson record or certificate linked to your session id, or for any other request, email clucknorrisapp@gmail.com and include your certificate id if you have one.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Its crypto-related educational content is intended for users of legal age in their jurisdiction.
